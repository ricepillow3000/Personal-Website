# Drum recording guide (electronic kit)

Record straight from the kit's line/headphone output into your laptop (Audacity is free).
Export everything as **WAV**; it gets converted to small `.ogg` files.

## 1. The loop (`loop.ogg`)
- Lo-fi city-night groove, **85 BPM**, play to a click track.
- **16 bars** (~45 seconds). Laid-back, a little swing, don't overplay — it sits under talking.
- Start and end on the beat so it loops seamlessly. Light fill in bar 16 is welcome.

## 2. Single hits (the click sounds)
Hit each piece **5 times**, leaving 2 seconds of silence between hits:

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| `hat` | Hover + the "talking" ticks (closed hi-hat, soft) |
| `snare` | Selecting a choice |
| `kick` | Going back / Esc |
| `crash` | CLEARED! stamps + achievements |
| `toms` | The contact card (a quick high-to-low tom fill) |

Send the WAVs over and they'll be trimmed, normalized and dropped into `public/assets/audio/`.
